How Encaixe works

Geometric puzzle with 1000 levels, for Android

The board has a shape: square, triangle, hexagon, star, heart… The pieces were cut from it. Drag, rotate and flip the pieces until they fill the whole shape, using as few moves as you can.

Fitting the pieces

  • Drag a piece onto the board. Near the right spot, it snaps into the grid; a shadow shows where it will land. If it touches the board and doesn't fit, it goes back.
  • Tap a piece to rotate it, or use the Rotate button.
  • Flip mirrors the piece. The button shows up in levels where the pieces come mirrored.
  • Any solution counts as long as every cell is covered by exactly one piece. Many shapes have more than one solution.

Moves and stars

  • 1 move is each rotation, each flip, each piece placed on the board and each piece taken off it. Arranging pieces off the board doesn't count.
  • The game works out the best solution: the one with the fewest moves.
  • 3 stars for matching the best solution; 2 for up to double; 1 for up to triple.
  • Limit: each game allows up to 3 times the moves of the best solution. The score shows how many you've used (for example, 7/24) and turns red near the end. If you reach the limit without solving it, the game ends and you just try again.
  • Hints: up to 3 per game. Each hint places a piece where the best solution puts it and counts its moves plus 2. With 1 or 2 hints, the most you can get is 2 stars; with 3, 1 star.
  • After solving, See best solution shows the solution with the fewest moves, step by step.

Levels

There are 1000 levels in 20 worlds of 50. Each level you solve unlocks the next.

  • The first ones teach you to drag, then to rotate and then to flip the pieces.
  • There are 22 shapes, on square or triangle grids: square, rectangle, staircase, pyramid, cross, letters L, T and U, frame, diamond, heart, arrow, house, lightning, triangle, hexagon, rhombus, trapezoid, star and hollow versions.
  • Pieces go from 3 to 14, and more and more of them come mirrored.
  • Every 10 levels there is a challenge, with 2 extra pieces.

Free mode

Pick the shape, the size (small, medium or large), the number of pieces and whether they come rotated and mirrored. Every game, Encaixe cuts a new puzzle and works out the best solution on the spot.

Language, about and privacy

The game follows your phone's language (English or Portuguese), and the menu has a button to switch. About and privacy shows the version, credits, a summary of the privacy policy, contact, open source licenses and a button to delete your progress.

Light and simple

The app works offline, has no ads or purchases and collects no data. Your stars and records are stored only on your device.
