Privacy policy

Encaixe · updated November 19, 2026 · Português

Encaixe is an Android puzzle game where you rotate, flip and fit pieces until they fill a geometric shape, developed by CjLabs, which is responsible for this policy. This page explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

No data is collected

The app does not collect, send or share any personal or usage data. It has no sign-up, no ads, no purchases, no analytics tools and no internet access.

What stays on your device

Your progress (the stars for each level, the fewest moves and the best time) the language and the sound on/off option are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. The app does not send this data anywhere.

To delete it, use the "Delete progress" button on the "About and privacy" screen, clear the app's data in Android's settings or uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app's only permission is vibration, used to give a short buzz when you pick up, fit or complete the pieces. Android grants this permission without asking, and it gives no access to any data. The app has no access to the internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or your files.

Links outside the app

On the "About and privacy" screen there is a link that opens this policy in your browser and another that opens your email app to contact CjLabs. They only work when you tap them; the app never sends anything on its own.

Children

The app can be used by people of all ages. Since no data is collected, it does not collect data from children either, and it has no ads or purchases.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this same page, with the updated date.

Contact

Encaixe is developed by CjLabs. For any questions about privacy or this policy, write to cirojosepadua@gmail.com.
