4eroes Privacy Policy

CjLabs · Last updated: November 15, 2026 · Português

In short: 4eroes does not collect, send or sell any personal data.
  • There is no sign-up, no account, no ads and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • What the game saves (your hero, your record and the sound volume) stays only on your device.
  • In multiplayer, devices talk directly to each other on the same Wi-Fi, without going through CjLabs servers.

1. Who we are

4eroes is a cooperative defense game for 1 to 4 players, developed by CjLabs, which is responsible for this policy. For any privacy question, write to cirojosepadua@gmail.com.

2. What is saved on your device

So you do not have to set everything up again every match, the game saves a few files in the app's private storage, on the device itself:

WhatWhy
Hero name, class, look, element and chosen alliesStart the next match with the same hero
Highest wave reached (record)Show your best result
Music and effects volume, sound on or offKeep your sound preferences

The game never sends this data out of the device. To erase it, clear the app's data in Android settings or uninstall the game.

The hero name is a nickname you choose. We recommend not using your full name or other personal data in it.

3. Matches on the same Wi-Fi

In Multiplayer, one device creates the match and the others join with a short code. This code is the local Wi-Fi address of the device that created the match, written in letters. It does not identify you on the internet.

During the match, the devices exchange directly only what is needed to play together: each hero's name and choices, movements, actions and the state of the match. None of this is sent to CjLabs or third parties, and nothing is kept after the match ends.

4. Android permissions

PermissionWhy the game asks for it
Network access (Internet)Connect devices on the same Wi-Fi network during multiplayer. The game does not access servers on the internet.
View network and Wi-Fi stateCheck whether the device is on Wi-Fi and find its local address, which becomes the match code.

The game does not ask for access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

5. Third-party services

4eroes does not use advertising, analytics or tracking libraries. The game is made with the Godot engine, which does not send data on its own.

If you download the game from an app store (such as Google Play), the store may process data about the download and installation under its own privacy policy, without CjLabs' involvement.

6. Children

The game can be played by people of all ages. Since we do not collect personal data from anyone, we do not collect data from children either. Parents and guardians can guide children to use a nickname instead of their real name for the hero.

7. Your rights

Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law No. 13,709/2018) and similar laws give you the right to know whether your data is processed and to ask for access, correction or deletion. Since 4eroes does not collect or store personal data outside your device, CjLabs holds no data about you. Even so, you can write to us at any time using the contact above.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game gains something that changes what is written here (for example, online matches, accounts or ads), this policy will be updated beforehand, with the new date at the top of the page, and important changes will be announced in the game itself.
