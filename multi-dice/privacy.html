Privacy policy

Multi Dice · updated November 16, 2026 · Português

Multi Dice is a dice roller app for Android, developed by CjLabs, which is responsible for this policy. This page explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

No data is collected

The app does not collect, send or share any personal or usage data. It has no sign-up, no ads, no purchases, no analytics tools and no internet access.

What stays on your device

Your settings (language, vibration, animation, shake to roll and the last dice you chose), the history of your last 50 rolls, the dice you create in "My dice" (names and faces) and your favorites are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. The app does not send this data anywhere.

To delete it, use the Clear button in the history and the delete buttons for dice and favorites, or uninstall the app.

Motion sensor

To roll the dice when you shake your phone, the app reads the accelerometer while it is open. This reading is used on the spot, only to recognize the shake, and is never stored or sent. The feature can be turned off in Settings.

Permissions

The app only uses the vibration permission, so the phone can vibrate when you roll. It can be turned off in Settings. The app does not ask for access to the internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or your files.

Children

Since no data is collected, the app does not collect data from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this same page, with the updated date.

Contact
