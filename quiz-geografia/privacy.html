Privacy policy

Universal Mundi Guesser (Geography Quiz) · last updated November 12, 2026 · Português

Universal Mundi Guesser is a country-guessing game for Android. This page explains what happens to your data when you use the game.

No data is collected

The game does not collect, send or share any personal or usage data. It has no sign-up, no ads, no analytics tools, and it does not access the internet.

What stays on your device

Your preferences (game mode, round types, continents, language, theme, sound, vibration and keyboard) and your progress (correct and wrong answers, statistics, game in progress, daily challenge, passport, achievements and the order of the next questions) are stored only on your device. The game does not send this data anywhere. If Android backup is turned on, the system itself may include it in your Google account backup, as it does for other apps; the developer has no access to that backup.

To delete it, use the Reset statistics button on the Statistics screen, or uninstall the game.

Sharing

The share buttons open Android's share sheet with a short text about your result (for example, how many stars you earned in the daily challenge). The text is only sent to the app you choose, and only if you choose to. The game receives nothing back.

Permissions

The game only uses the vibration permission, so the phone can vibrate on right and wrong answers. It can be turned off in the game's Settings. The game does not ask for access to the internet, location, contacts, camera or files.

Children

Since no data is collected, the game does not collect data from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this same page, with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer's contact email, shown on the Universal Mundi Guesser page on Google Play.
