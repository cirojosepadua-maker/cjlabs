Privacy policy

Quero Dizer · last updated November 15, 2026 · Português

Quero Dizer ("I want to say") is a free Augmentative and Alternative Communication (AAC) app for Android: the child taps pictures, builds a sentence and the device speaks it. This page explains what happens to data when you use the app.

No data is collected

The app does not collect, send or share any personal or usage data. It has no sign-up, no ads, no purchases and no analytics, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on the device

On first launch, the app asks for the child's name (optional) and which character appears in the pictures (Léo or Bia). This information, the settings (speak on tap and calm mode) and the steps checked in today's routine are stored only on the device. The app never sends them anywhere and excludes them from Android's automatic backup.

To delete them, change or erase the name in Settings, use "Clear data" for the app in Android's settings, or uninstall the app.

Voice

To speak words and sentences, the app uses the device's own text-to-speech engine, which Android provides to all apps. Quero Dizer does not record audio, does not use the microphone and does not keep what was spoken.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or your files.

Children

Quero Dizer is made to be used by children and set up by adults (parents, therapists and teachers). Since no data is collected, the app does not collect data from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with the updated date.
